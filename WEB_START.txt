HUONG DAN BAT DAU - SAN CHUNG KHOAN WEB 0.2
===========================================

A. CAI BAN WEB MOI
1. Giai nen san_chung_khoan_web_full.zip vao mot thu muc moi.
2. Chay install_windows.bat va doi den khi bao cai dat hoan tat.

B. GIU TAI KHOAN OWNER DA TAO O BAN THU NGHIEM 0.1
1. Quay lai cua so CMD dang chay ban cu va nhan Ctrl+C de dung server.
2. Mo thu muc ban cu: san_chung_khoan_web_tai_lai\san_chung_khoan_web.
3. Copy nguyen thu muc data.
4. Paste thu muc data do vao san_chung_khoan_web_full.
5. Chon gop/ghi de neu Windows hoi; khong xoa ban cu.
6. Khong chay create_owner_windows.bat neu da copy database, vi Owner cu da ton tai.

C. NEU MUON BAT DAU DATABASE MOI
1. Khong copy data cua ban cu.
2. Chay create_owner_windows.bat.
3. Tu dat ten dang nhap va mat khau rieng cho Owner; khong gui mat khau cho bat ky ai.

D. KHOI DONG
1. Chay run_windows.bat.
2. Cho den khi CMD hien Uvicorn running.
3. Mo Chrome/Edge tai http://127.0.0.1:8001.
4. Dang nhap bang tai khoan Owner da giu lai hoac tai khoan Owner moi.
5. Giu cua so CMD mo trong khi su dung web.

DUNG SERVER
- Quay lai cua so CMD chay server va nhan Ctrl+C.
- Khong xoa thu muc data neu muon giu tai khoan, so du, giao dich, chat va lich su.

QUAN TRONG
- Ban 0.1 van duoc giu nguyen, chay tren cong 8000.
- Ban 0.2 chay tren cong 8001 va dung database rieng trong thu muc data.
- Chi sao chep thu muc data; khong di chuyen hay xoa ban cu.
- Ban Pygame va ban web khong tu dong dong bo accounts_sandbox.json.
- Day la san mo phong, chi dung USD ao.


E. RENDER FREE — CHI THU NGHIEM
1. Doc RENDER_FREE_TRIAL.txt truoc.
2. Khong tai len database cu hoac dung mat khau Owner local.
3. Khi tao Blueprint tren Render, nhap INITIAL_OWNER_USERNAME va INITIAL_OWNER_PASSWORD bang mat khau demo rieng.
4. Chi su dung tai khoan demo; du lieu co the mat khi dich vu ngu hoac khoi dong lai.
5. Khong coi Render Free la dich vu 24/7 va khong dua vao do de luu du lieu quan trong.
